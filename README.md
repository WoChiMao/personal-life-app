# 🏠 Personal Life App

> One app that helps you remember, organize, and manage the important things in life.

## 🎯 Vision

The goal of this project is to create a Personal Life Management App that becomes a central place for managing the important responsibilities of everyday life.

Instead of using separate apps for tasks, reminders, bills, documents, family responsibilities, and other important information, the app aims to bring these areas together into one simple and intelligent experience.

### Core Promise

> **Never forget the important things in life.**
